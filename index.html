Where the lake meets island life

Bufumira Island is a peaceful destination on Lake Victoria, Uganda. It offers visitors an opportunity to experience nature, lake views, local community life, fishing, camping and beautiful island sunsets.

Our vision is to develop Bufumira into a responsible tourism destination while creating opportunities for local people and protecting the natural environment.
