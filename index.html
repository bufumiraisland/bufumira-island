A Small Island With a Bigger Vision

Bufumira Island is more than a beautiful destination. We are working toward a future where people can build livelihoods while protecting the island's natural environment and culture.

Our long-term vision is to create a community-led model that connects conservation, sustainable agriculture, youth skills, responsible eco-tourism and community wellbeing.

Community-Led

Development shaped around local people, local needs and local opportunities.

Nature First

Protecting land, trees, water and the lake environment for future generations.

Sustainable Livelihoods

Creating practical opportunities through agriculture, skills and responsible tourism.

HELP US BUILD PHASE 1

We are seeking partners, donors and supporters to help build a healthier, greener and more resilient future for Bufumira Island. Our priorities begin with essential community needs and continue toward conservation, education, skills and responsible community-based tourism.

Our Initial Goal

Phase 1 community development, conservation and eco-tourism fund

US$10,000

💧 Borehole Water Project

Help assess and develop a reliable source of safe fresh water for island households and community activities, subject to technical assessment, feasibility and required permissions.

Priority: access to safe water

🚻 Toilets & Latrines

Support suitable, safe and hygienic toilet or latrine facilities to improve dignity, public health and sanitation for residents and visitors.

Priority: safe sanitation

♻️ Waste Management

Help establish practical waste collection, sorting and disposal arrangements that reduce pollution and protect the island and Lake Victoria environment.

Priority: a cleaner island

🌱 Tree Planting & Conservation

Continue planting trees, restoring cleared areas and encouraging community action to protect the island's natural environment.

Priority: environmental protection

📚 Education & Girls' Support

Help children access essential scholastic materials and support girls' menstrual health, dignity and participation in education.

Priority: learning and wellbeing

🧵 Women's Skills

Support practical skills development that can help women build confidence, pursue their goals and develop small income-generating activities.

Priority: women's empowerment

🛠 Youth Hands-on Skills

Help young people access practical training and experience that can lead to work, self-employment and local enterprise opportunities.

Priority: skills and livelihoods

☀️ Solar Energy & ICT

Help provide solar-powered lighting and, as resources allow, basic ICT access and learning opportunities in a place with limited electricity.

Priority: learning and access

🏕 Tourism, Camping & Eco-Cottages

Promote the island's natural and cultural experiences and gradually develop responsible camping and future eco-cottage facilities that can create local income.

Priority: responsible tourism

🤝 Community Livelihoods

Help local people benefit from visitors through guiding, boat services, food, crafts, agriculture and other community-provided goods and services.

Priority: income for local people
